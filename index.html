Who am I

Hello! I go by Kene. (Think Penne with a K, so if you're suddenly hungry, blame my parents.)

The rest of this site will give you the breakdown of my professional history, but here is who I am at my core: someone who loves understanding people and solving meaningful problems.

The most rewarding moments of my career have come from sitting down with customers and cross-functional teammates, listening to their frustrations, uncovering the actual root causes, and building solutions that make their lives noticeably easier. That innate curiosity and empathy are what ultimately led me into product management.

What drives me:

  • User-First Mentality: The best products are built by people who care deeply about the users they serve.
  • Continuous Improvement: Every conversation is a chance to learn; every problem is a chance to optimize.

When I'm offline:

You'll usually find me following Chelsea FC, talking about cars, discovering new music, or enjoying the beauty nature has to offer. Whether I'm reverse-engineering how a car engine works or failing spectacularly at a pottery wheel, I just love learning how things are put together, and how to make them work even better.

04
Provider Data Pipeline
DATA ENGINEERING
DATA PIPELINE • Python, SQL, SQLite, REST API
SQL validation output flagging stale and incomplete provider records.

Data Quality Gap

Healthcare provider directories are pulled from public registries, but raw records often carry stale entries, missing fields, and duplicates that make them unreliable to hand to a client as-is. The goal was to build a pipeline that ingests, validates, and exports provider data on a schedule instead of by hand.

Solution

Rather than trust registry data as-is, I treated validation as the core deliverable, not a bolt-on. Ingestion normalizes records from the NPI API into a relational schema; 14 SQL rules then score each one for validity, freshness, and duplication before it reaches a client. Config-driven exports let each client define their own fields and filters, with a Claude skill for fast triage when something looks off.

Impact

  • Loaded 601 unique Colorado providers from 6 API searches with zero data-integrity errors.
  • Flagged 292 stale records (5+ years unupdated) and 18 missing credentials via automated SQL rules.
  • Diagnosed and fixed a specialty-name mismatch causing a silent empty export.
